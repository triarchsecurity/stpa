# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Argue about the work, not the person.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Direct, specific technical criticism — of an analysis, a finding, or a line of code
- Assuming the other person read the thing and reached a different conclusion honestly
- Accepting a refutation when the evidence is good

Examples of behaviour that does not:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments
- Publishing findings against a system whose owner has not agreed to it
- Sustained derailing after a decision has been made and explained

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **security@triarchsecurity.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, and will say why.
